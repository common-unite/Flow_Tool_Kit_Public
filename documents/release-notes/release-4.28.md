# Release 4.28

**Payment Required** on a Form Template now does what its help text has always promised: a payment-required form saves as **Pending Payment** on submit, collects payment, and only completes once payment succeeds.

- **Payment Required Form Templates, base half** (#612): checking **Payment Required** on a Form Template now gates completion behind a paid step. On submit the response is saved with the new **Pending Payment** status and no Submission Date, the payment component appears in place of the form (with the stage indicator showing a locked **Payment** step and no Back, Next or Submit), and on successful payment the response is updated with the payment details, marked **Submitted**, stamped with its Submission Date, and the normal confirmation message appears. Nothing downstream runs early: the conversion process and the confirmation email both wait for the Submitted transition, so an abandoned or declined payment creates no Contact, Account, Opportunity or Campaign Member and sends no email. A respondent who returns to a Pending Payment response is taken straight back to the payment step. In a Screen Flow the form holds Next/Finish until payment succeeds.
- **Requires the Stripe Connector Accelerator, in a release that is still in progress.** Form Builder alone cannot take a payment. **Do not enable Payment Required until the paired Stripe Connector release ships**: with the current Stripe Connector installed, the payment component cannot start when hosted by a form and respondents would see an empty payment page. With no Stripe Connector installed at all, a respondent instead sees a clear "Payment Unavailable" message. Both are safe to leave configured for testing; neither takes a payment.
- **New Payment Settings section** on the Form Template Settings page surfaces **Payment Required** with the prerequisite notice and a link to the Stripe Connector Accelerator setup documentation. The amount charged comes from **Stripe Amount**, calculated from the response's **Amount** field. On any payment form, check **Trigger Formula Recalculations** on the field that drives the amount, so the payment step never renders against a stale value.
- **New Status value on upgraded orgs**: **Pending Payment** is written by the form regardless, but managed picklist values do not propagate to orgs that upgrade. If you want it selectable in list views, reports and record edits, add **Pending Payment** to **Form Submission → Status** in Setup after upgrading. Fresh installs get it automatically.
- **Translatable payment copy**: the payment step's indicator label and its three respondent-facing messages are custom labels (`Payment_Step`, `Payment_Unavailable`, `Payment_Package_Missing`, `Payment_Gate_Unverifiable`) under the **Form** category in **Setup → Translate**.

Admins: load your public form pages, including any embedded form URLs, once after upgrading; the first visit pays the component compile so a real visitor does not.
