# Release 4.27

Two fixed-English messages become translatable, a false error toast disappears, and embedded payment forms can offer digital wallets.

- **"Selection Required" and the multiselect limit text now translate** (#610, #611): the Date/Time (Calendar/Scheduler) component's "Selection Required" pop-up title, and the Multiselect checkbox question's helper text ("Select up to (N) options" and its at-least / between / exact variants) and validation messages, were hardcoded in English with no label to translate. They now read from custom labels. Translate them in **Setup → Translate** (Setup Component: **Custom Label**): the title is `Error_Selection_Required_Title`, and the multiselect text is the `Multiselect_Options_*` and `Multiselect_Error_*` labels under the **Form Builder, Multiselect** category.
- **No more "Form Load Error" toast on a form that works** (#603): community and guest users permissioned through a cloned profile or a snapshot of a permission set (rather than the live permission set) could see a "Form Load Error / The requested resource does not exist" toast on every Form Template page load, even though the form rendered and submitted fine. The lookups behind it are cosmetic button labels and now fall back to their defaults silently. Admins: assign the **Form Flow User** permission set to your community and guest profiles rather than a one-time copy of its permissions, so a future field addition does not reopen this.
- **Cleaner prefill loading** (#605): removed the "Preparing your form" text beside the prefill spinner.
- **Digital wallets in embedded payment forms** (#595): the generated embed snippet now delegates the `payment` permission to the iframe, so Google Pay, Apple Pay and Express Checkout work inside an embedded payment form. Existing embeds keep working; regenerate the snippet only if you want wallets in an embedded payment form.

Admins: load your public form pages, including any embedded form URLs, once after upgrading; the first visit pays the component compile so a real visitor does not.
